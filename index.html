A cappella, all season long

Join Central Standard and Vocal Standard, two internationally acclaimed barbershop choruses, for their annual holiday show at the historic Folly Theater in downtown Kansas City. Both performances are filled with stunning a cappella harmony, plus several chapter quartets along the way.

Audiences are treated to a festive mix of secular and sacred holiday music that's sure to delight the whole family. It's a magical musical tradition in one of Kansas City's most iconic venues.
