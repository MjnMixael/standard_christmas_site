A cappella, all season long

Central Standard and Vocal Standard, two internationally acclaimed barbershop choruses, come together each December for their annual holiday show. Expect rich four-part harmony, a festive mix of secular and sacred favorites, and several chapter quartets along the way.

It's an afternoon — and evening — the whole family will love, in one of Kansas City's most beloved historic theaters.
